# Local Directory Listings & Backlink Checklist

For RANLOCK Roofing & Construction LLC. This is a manual execution checklist —
there's no API/connector access in this environment to create these listings
directly, so each line below is something a human (owner or staff) submits
through that site's own form. Budget ~2-3 per week rather than all at once;
rankings move over months, not days, and a burst of identical new listings in
one day can read as spammy to some directories.

**Zero-ad-spend rule:** the goal is traffic/leads without marketing dollars,
so every item below is tagged **🆓 Free** or **💰 Paid**. Default to the free
items only. Paid items (Data Axle, Neustar Localeze, NRCA membership, chamber
dues, BBB accreditation, Angi/Thumbtack/HomeAdvisor paid tiers) are listed for
completeness but are **optional/deferred**, not part of the default plan —
revisit them only if/when you decide to spend on growth deliberately, not as
a default next step.

## 1. Canonical NAP (Name / Address / Phone) — copy exactly, every time

Inconsistent formatting across directories (abbreviating "Suite" as "Ste",
dropping the suite number, using a different phone format) actively hurts
local SEO. Use this exact block everywhere:

```
RANLOCK Roofing & Construction LLC
1322 Space Park Drive, Suite C113
Houston, TX 77058
(281) 299-1086
https://ranlock.com
```

Category/business type to select wherever a directory asks: **Roofing
Contractor** (primary), with Siding, Window Installation, Gutters, and
General Contractor as secondary categories where the platform allows more
than one.

Service area: list every city you already have a landing page for —
Houston, Baytown, Clear Lake, Cypress, Deer Park, Friendswood, League City,
Pasadena, Pearland, Spring, Sugar Land, Texas City, The Woodlands, Tomball,
Webster.

## 2. Directory listings — priority order

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google Business Profile | 🆓 Free | Already flagged as highest-priority separately — do this first if not done | — |

| 8 | Nextdoor Business Page | 🆓 Free | Hyper-local, good for referral-driven neighborhoods (HOAs, subdivisions) | — |
| 9 | Facebook Business Page | 🆓 Free | Not a directory per se, but counts as a citation + review surface | Keep NAP block identical here too |

Do not claim licenses, certifications, warranties, or "accredited" status on
any of these profiles unless it's actually true and current — several
directories (BBB especially) verify this and a false claim can get a listing
suspended.

## 3. Additional directories — second wave

Beyond the priority-1 list above, these add incremental citation/backlink
value. Lower individual impact each, but they compound, and several of them
(the data aggregators especially) feed dozens of smaller directories
automatically from one submission.

| Directory | Cost | Why it matters | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Superpages | 🆓 Free | Same category as YP | — |
| MapQuest | 🆓 Free | Still used by some map/navigation integrations | — |
| Manta | 🆓 Free | Small business directory, decent domain authority | — |
| Foursquare | 🆓 Free | Feeds some map/location apps | — |

| BuildZoom | 🆓 Free | Home-services/contractor directory, shows permit history in some markets | — |

For the commercial/B2B side specifically (property managers, HOAs, GCs,
facilities teams — RANLOCK's other target segments per the outreach skill):
municipal and property-management **vendor registration portals** are worth
pursuing opportunistically — 🆓 free (time/outreach only). A `.gov` or large
property-management company's "approved vendor" listing is both a lead
source and a very high-authority backlink, though these are typically
invite/registration-only rather than self-serve — treat as outreach, not a
quick signup.

## 4. Backlink targets (earned links, not directories)

These move domain authority more than directory citations do, but take
real outreach — no shortcut:

- **Local chambers of commerce** 💰 Paid dues (~$150-500/yr) — Houston, Bay
  Area Houston (Clear Lake/Webster area), Pearland, League City, Sugar Land,
  The Woodlands. **Skip for now** given the zero-spend goal; the member
  directory backlink is real, but it's a deliberate spend decision, not a
  default action.
- **Manufacturer "find a contractor" pages** — RANLOCK is already listed on both:
  - **GAF Certified Contractor** (not Master Elite — do not use "Master Elite" anywhere in copy or badges) — already listed on GAF's "Find a Contractor" directory. Since the listing already exists, the remaining SEO work is just making sure ranlock.com itself links out to that GAF directory listing (and/or references the GAF Certified Contractor badge on relevant roofing pages, using only GAF's current approved logo/usage guidelines for that specific tier), so the backlink relationship is visible in both directions.
  - **Owens Corning Preferred Contractor** — already listed on Owens Corning's contractor locator (confirmed tier: Preferred, not Platinum — use "Preferred Contractor" in all badge/copy references, not "Platinum"). Same follow-up: link to that listing from ranlock.com and use the correct Preferred-tier badge.
  - Do not add CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster or any other manufacturer program RANLOCK doesn't hold.
  - Re-verify both listings annually at renewal time, since manufacturer certifications lapse if not renewed.
- **Local news / sponsorships** 🆓 Free-to-cheap (some local sponsorships
  have a small cost, but press coverage itself is free) — local news coverage
  of a community-visible project (storm response, a notable before/after)
  is a strong, naturally-earned link with zero spend; a paid team/booster
  club sponsorship is optional, not required for this to work.
- **Industry associations** 💰 Paid membership — National Association of the
  Remodeling Industry (NARI) Houston chapter, Greater Houston Builders
  Association. **Skip for now**, same reasoning as NRCA/chambers above.
- **Supplier/partner pages** 🆓 Free — any roofing material supplier or
  distributor RANLOCK buys from regularly may list contractor customers/
  partners on their site at no cost; worth a direct ask.
- **Insurance/public-adjuster partners** 🆓 Free — if RANLOCK already has
  referral relationships here (per the commercial-growth skill's target
  categories), ask if a mutual-partner page/link makes sense; this is
  relationship/outreach time, not a paid listing.

## 6. Tracking

Add a simple tracker (new tab in the existing prospect-tracking Sheet, or a
new one) with columns: `directory_name, url, date_submitted, status
(pending/live/rejected), login_email_used, notes`. This avoids duplicate
submissions and gives a quick audit trail of what's live.
